Pokemon Stadium Recomp - online play (test build)
=================================================

2 to 4 players, each on their own PC, over the internet. One player hosts;
everyone else joins the host (only the host forwards a port).

Before you start (both players)
- Everyone needs their own Pokemon Stadium (USA) v1.0 ROM. No ROMs are
  included. Later revisions ("Rev 1", "Rev 2") don't work.
- Run PokemonStadiumRecomp.exe and choose your ROM in the launcher.
- Both players must use this exact same download. A different build is
  refused.
- Online games use rental Pokemon for now: any Transfer Pak carts you set
  up stay unplugged during online play.

Host (player 1)
1. Forward TCP port 7311 on your router to this PC. If Windows Firewall
   asks, allow it on Private networks.
2. Find your public IP address (search "what is my IP") and send it to
   the others.
3. In the launcher's ONLINE PLAY card, choose Host, pick how many Players
   (2-4), and press Play. A "Hosting" message shows while you wait; the
   game starts when everyone has joined.

Guests (players 2-4, numbered in the order they join)
1. In the launcher's ONLINE PLAY card, choose Join and type the host's
   address.
2. Press Play. A "Connecting" message shows; it keeps trying for a minute
   while the host gets ready.

The launcher remembers these settings. Choose Off to play on your own again.

During the game
- Each player uses their own controller (whatever the launcher sets as
  player 1 on their PC).
- Guests play on a copy of the host's Stadium save; their own saves
  aren't changed.
- In battles two players fight; players 3-4 can swap in for the next
  match. Kids Club mini-games use all four.
- The input delay is set automatically from the connection, so on a slow
  or jittery connection your presses take effect a few frames later. If
  one game is slow, everyone waits for it. That's normal.
- "The games went out of sync" means a desync bug. Please report what
  you were doing.
- Closing any window ends the session for everyone.

If something goes wrong
- Each player has a netplay.log next to PokemonStadiumRecomp.exe. Send
  everyone's logs along with what you were doing (freeze, crash,
  out-of-sync message, slowness).
