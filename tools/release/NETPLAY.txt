Pokemon Stadium Recomp - netplay (test build)
=============================================

Two players, each on their own PC, over the internet.

Before you start (both players)
- Everyone needs their own Pokemon Stadium (USA) v1.0 ROM. No ROMs are
  included. Later revisions ("Rev 1", "Rev 2") don't work.
- Run PokemonStadiumRecomp.exe once and choose your ROM in the launcher.
- Both players must use this exact same download. A different build is
  refused.
- No Transfer Pak carts yet: leave the Transfer Pak settings empty. Use
  rental Pokemon for now.

Host (player 1)
1. Forward TCP port 7311 on your router to this PC. If Windows Firewall
   asks, allow it on Private networks.
2. Find your public IP address (search "what is my IP") and send it to
   the other player.
3. Double-click "Netplay - Host.bat" and press Play in the launcher. A
   "Hosting" message shows while you wait; the game starts when the other
   player joins.

Guest (player 2)
1. Double-click "Netplay - Join.bat" and type the host's address.
2. Press Play in the launcher. A "Connecting" message shows; it keeps
   trying for a minute while the host gets ready.

During the game
- Each player uses their own controller (whatever the launcher sets as
  player 1 on their PC).
- The guest plays on a copy of the host's Stadium save; the guest's own
  save isn't changed.
- If one game is slow, both wait for it. That's normal.
- "The two games went out of sync" means a desync bug. Please report what
  you were doing.
- Closing either window ends the session for both.
